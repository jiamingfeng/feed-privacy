Feed

A simple, private Android app for tracking newborn breastfeeding, pumping, and diapers — built by a new dad, for exhausted parents.

Local-only No account No ads Free & open

What it does

Log every feeding session in one place — diaper, breast time on each side, formula top-up, pumped volume, and a note — then look back at the data to spot patterns: feeding frequency, intake, output, and weight-gain rhythm. Everything stays on your phone.

One-tap logging

A 4-step session wizard — diaper, feed, pump, review — where every step is skippable.

Reminders that wake you

Schedules a real alarm through your phone's Clock app, ringing through Do Not Disturb.

See the trend

A 24-hour timeline strip, daily totals, and a pumping chart with a rolling average.

Your data stays yours

Local SQLite storage. Export and import as JSON anytime. No cloud, no tracking.
